Top 15 Ladder

Fifteen places, and the only way up is to take someone else’s. Every match is a race to five — no spots, no handicaps. Starts Monday, November 30. See the rules below — new this year, you can challenge up to two spots. Please report your wins on this page, and use our Facebook page to set up challenges.

How it works ▸ The rules
  • 1The ladder started with the top 15 shooting averages in the league. After that it’s won and lost on the table — league stats don’t move anybody.
  • 2You may challenge one or two places above you, no further. Win and you take their place — anybody in between slides down one. Lose and nothing moves.
  • 3Not on the ladder yet? You can challenge #14 or #15. Win and you take their place — they come off the ladder and have to work their way back on the same way.
  • 4Put the challenge out on our Facebook page or straight to the player. They have 48 hours to answer and get a night in the book, or they forfeit the place.
  • 5Every match is a race to 5. Ratings don’t matter.
  • 6The loser pays $20: $15 to the winner, $5 into the envelope.
  • 7The envelope is paid out at the end of every month, starting December 31. It goes to whoever is sitting at #1 that day — twenty matches in a month is $100 on top of the ladder.
